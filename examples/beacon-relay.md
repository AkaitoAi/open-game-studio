# Beacon Relay production brief

Build an original 5–8 minute 3D exploration puzzle for desktop. The starter includes a small primitive-based pickup and delivery loop as a reference. Preserve a working build while improving it.

## Player experience

A maintenance robot retrieves a power core, carries it to a mountaintop relay and aligns the dish. A clear signal and readable landmarks guide the player. Finish with a short beacon activation sequence and a restart option.

## Output contracts

- Producer delivers a scoped design and acceptance criteria.
- Gameplay and world roles deliver reviewable project changes.
- Models delivers one modular environment kit and one original hero prop or robot, with source, import derivative, triangle report, and provenance.
- UI delivers editable artwork, exported icons, menu states, and text and focus checks.
- Audio delivers a spatial relay cue, pickup, socket, ambience, and an event-to-file cue map.
- Integrator records the exact project revision, asset versions, and build path.
- QA records the engine smoke result, graphical playthrough, restart and negative paths, and unresolved failures.
- Marketing delivers draft store copy, capture list, Photoshop capsule variants, and a provenance and AI disclosure draft.

Test swap-in of a new core mesh, adjustment of the objective HUD, and a softer relay cue. Verify the same playthrough still passes. Use real build captures for marketing. Keep all publication steps for human review.
