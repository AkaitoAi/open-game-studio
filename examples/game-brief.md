# Your game's first playable

- **Premise:** Describe the player and their goal.
- **Core loop:** Describe what the player repeatedly does.
- **Scope:** Pick one level, one main mechanic, and a clear ending.
- **Controls and platform:** Specify supported input and where the build runs.
- **Art and audio:** Describe the desired look and sound, plus any assets you already own.
- **Acceptance checks:** Describe how to reproduce and verify the gameplay loop and restart.
- **Later:** List features to leave for a future build.

For an existing project, identify the starting scene, relevant systems, and what the agent should preserve.
